The **Hopping Spider Nest** houses the spiders that move items around a [Web Network](@critterworks:web_networks).

Nests generate rarely in underground Overworld caves with several spiders and a small network already inside.

## Managing Spiders

Use a **Hopping Spider** item on the nest to add it. Sneak and interact with an empty hand to remove the most recently added spider. Custom spider names are kept when spiders enter or leave the nest.

Interact with the nest to view each spider's position and current behavior. The screen shows whether a spider is wandering, working, waiting to deliver, or unable to finish a delivery.

Breaking the nest drops its spiders and anything they were carrying.
