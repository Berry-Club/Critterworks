**Scoochworm Apples** are small structures found in Lush Caves. Each apple contains a berry-bearing Cave Vine, a vine of [Scoochstem](@critterworks:scoochstem) on top, and generates with a [Scoochworm](@critterworks:scoochworm) inside.

The apple's shell is made from Apple Slice blocks. Breaking a slice normally drops 1–3 Apples, with Fortune increasing the yield. Silk Touch drops the Apple Slice itself instead.

One Apple Slice can be crafted into nine Apples.

## Configuration

By default, the game attempts to generate a Scoochworm Apple about once every 24 Lush Cave chunks. This rarity can be configured.
