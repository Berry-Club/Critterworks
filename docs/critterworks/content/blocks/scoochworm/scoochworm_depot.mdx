The **Scoochworm Depot** is a Redstone-controlled stop for [Scoochworms](@critterworks:scoochworm).

Scoochworms can always travel onto a Depot, but can only *exit* the Depot if it's unpowered.

Use Depots to hold Scoochworms at loading stations, intersections, or other places where their movement needs to be controlled automatically, especially in combination with [Stem-Encased Comparators](@critterworks:stem_encased_comparator).
