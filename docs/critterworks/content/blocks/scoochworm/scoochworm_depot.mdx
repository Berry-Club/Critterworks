The **Scoochworm Depot** is a Redstone-controlled stop for [Scoochworms](@critterworks:scoochworms).

A Scoochworm can travel over an unpowered Depot like an ordinary [Scoochstem](@critterworks:scoochstem). While the Depot receives a Redstone signal, a Scoochworm on it cannot leave. Removing the signal lets the Scoochworm continue along its route.

Use Depots to hold Scoochworms at loading stations, intersections, or other places where their movement needs to be controlled automatically.

The Scoochworm Depot is currently only available from the Creative inventory.
