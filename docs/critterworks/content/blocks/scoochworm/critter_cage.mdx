The **Critter Cage** can trap most living entities and contain them in item form. An entity cannot be captured if its type is in the `#c:capturing_not_supported` entity type tag. Captured entities keep their data, such as a [Scoochworm's](@critterworks:scoochworm) length, color, and attachments.

Use the filled cage on a block to release its entity directly; sneak while placing it to place the filled cage as a block instead.

## Automatic Capture and Release

Place an open cage facing the end of a Scoochworm's route. When the Scoochworm reaches the cage and cannot continue forward, it enters the cage. Other entities must be captured by using the cage on them.

Interact with a filled cage to release its entity. A Redstone signal also releases it when the cage changes from unpowered to powered. The space in front of the cage must be clear.
