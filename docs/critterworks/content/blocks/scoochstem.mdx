**Scoochstem** and **Scoochstem Wood** form paths for Scoochworms across floors, walls, ceilings, and corners. A wandering Scoochworm begins following a path when it reaches one.

Both variants behave as logs: they can be placed along any axis, burn, and are mined most effectively with an axe. Regular Scoochstem has exposed ends, while Scoochstem Wood is bark-covered on every face.

Scoochworms travel along the faces of the blocks, so leave enough room around bends for their bodies. At a branch, they continue forward when possible, otherwise choosing an available side path.

## Disabling Faces

Use an **Axe** on a Scoochstem to toggle the clicked face. A disabled face cannot support Scoochworm travel and uses a darker texture.

Each face can be toggled independently.
