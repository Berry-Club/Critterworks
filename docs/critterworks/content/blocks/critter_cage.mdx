The **Critter Cage** captures and transports an entire [Scoochworm](@critterworks:scoochworm), including its color, segments, and attachments.

Use an empty cage on a Scoochworm to capture it. A filled cage glints and keeps the Scoochworm stored when the cage is broken or carried. Use the filled cage on a block to release the Scoochworm directly; sneak while placing it to place the filled cage as a block instead.

## Automatic Capture and Release

Place an open cage facing the end of a Scoochworm's route. When the Scoochworm reaches the cage and cannot continue forward, it enters the cage.

Interact with a filled cage to release its Scoochworm. A Redstone signal also releases it when the cage changes from unpowered to powered. The space in front of the cage must be clear.

The Critter Cage is currently only available from the Creative inventory.
