The **Sparkpollen Pouch** marks the blocks that a [Sparkbug Bush](@critterworks:sparkbug_bush) uses to transfer FE.

Use the pouch on a Sparkbug Bush to bind it. Once bound, use it on a face of a block with an FE connection to add a Sparkpollen Point for that face.

Sneak and use the pouch in the air to switch between its two modes:

| Mode | Color | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charging | Blue | The bush extracts FE from the marked face. |
| Grounding | Orange | The bush inserts FE into the marked face. |

Use the pouch on an already marked face to remove that point. Hold a pouch bound to the bush to see its points.
