A **Pearl** has a 10% chance to be produced whenever a [Filterfeeder](@critterworks:filterfeeders) is fed any type of Sand.

Pearls are used to tame [Punchprawns](@critterworks:punchprawn). Holding one also prevents wild Punchprawns from targeting you.
