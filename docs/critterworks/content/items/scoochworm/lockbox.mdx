The **Lockbox** is a [Scoochworm](@critterworks:scoochworm) attachment that allows the Scoochworm segment it's placed on to store up to 27 stacks of items.

As the Scoochworm's head passes over items, it will pick them up, storing them in Lockboxes front-to-back. While the Lockbox's segment is upside down, the Lockbox will open, dropping its contents into the world. With these two mechanics, you can fully automate item insertion and extraction from the Scoochworm.

Sneak and interact with an empty hand to remove the Lockbox. Its contents remain inside the item when it is removed or dropped.
