The **Critter Catcher** is a reusable tool for catching certain creatures that can also exist as items.

Use it on an adult [Filterfeeder](@critterworks:filterfeeders) or [Parry Platypus](@critterworks:parry_platypus) to turn the creature into its corresponding item. Custom names and item data are preserved.

Baby creatures cannot be caught. A tameable creature can only be caught by its owner.
