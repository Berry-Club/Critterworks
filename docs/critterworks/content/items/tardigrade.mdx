A **Tardigrade** can be attached to almost any item to protect it while it exists as a dropped item entity.

Craft one Tardigrade with one item stack to attach it. The protected item displays a Tardigrade icon and becomes immune to damage types in `#critterworks:tardigrade_protects_from`. By default, this includes environmental damage.

Craft a protected item with a regular **Potion of Poison** to remove the Tardigrade.

## Configuration

The server config can toggle whether or not Tardigrade protection also prevents the item from expiring.

Data packs can change the damage types that Tardigrades protect against by editing `#critterworks:tardigrade_protects_from`.
