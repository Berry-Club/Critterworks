The **Snail Shield** protects its user by enclosing them in a shell. Hold it in either hand and sneak to activate it.

While shelled, you cannot take damage or be knocked back, and your size and movement speed are greatly reduced. Each blocked hit consumes one point of the shield's 256 durability. You also cannot attack or use items while the shell is active.

Remain motionless for five seconds to hide completely. Most nearby mobs forget you and cannot target you again until you move or leave the shell. Data packs can add exceptions with the `#critterworks:can_target_fully_shelled_players` entity type tag.

Repair a Snail Shield with [Snail Shell Fragments](@critterworks:snail_shell_fragment), which are dropped by [Sproutsnails](@critterworks:sproutsnail).
