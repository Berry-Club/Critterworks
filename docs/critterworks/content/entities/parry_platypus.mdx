**Parry Platypeople** are amphibious creatures found in and around rivers. When held as an item, they can be used in a way similar to Shields.

Use a [Critter Catcher](@critterworks:critter_catcher) on an adult Parry Platypus to catch it as an item. A dropped Platypus item will turn back into a mob after 5 seconds.
