**Sproutsnails** are peaceful creatures found in swamp biomes. They are attracted to Mushrooms and seek out nearby blocks that can be grown with Bone Meal.

When a Sproutsnail enters a new block, it has a 20% chance to apply a Bone Meal effect if that block can grow. Adults passively drop [Snail Slime](@critterworks:snail_slime) every five to ten minutes.

A Sproutsnail killed by a player has a 10% chance to drop a [Snail Shell Fragment](@critterworks:snail_shell_fragment). There is also a 1% chance that its death begins a visit from [The Snail That Kills You](@critterworks:evil_snail), unless that player is already being pursued.

The growth chance and pursuing snail's chance and speed can be configured by server owners.
