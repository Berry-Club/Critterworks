**Punchprawns** are amphibious creatures that spawn in and around Ocean Monuments. Wild Punchprawns are hostile, but will not attack a player holding a [Pearl](@critterworks:pearl).

## Taming

Interact with a wild Punchprawn using a Pearl for a 20% chance to tame it. Tamed Punchprawns follow their owner on land and through water. Interact with your Punchprawn using an empty hand to tell it to sit or follow.

Sneak and interact with your tamed Punchprawn using an empty hand to wear it on your head. Interact with a block to place it back into the world.

## Punching

While wearing a Punchprawn, a fully charged bare-handed melee attack deals 14 additional damage and applies strong knockback. The Punchprawn takes ten seconds to recharge by default.

The damage, knockback, and recharge time can be configured by server owners.
