Filterfeeders are an animal that can be found swimming along the bottom of oceans and rivers. Swimming near one will clear your vision of water fog, allowing you to see farther.

They come in two types: [Item Filterfeeders](@critterworks:item_filterfeeder) and [Fluid Filterfeeders](@critterworks:fluid_filterfeeder). Interacting with one will catch it, converting it into the appropriate item. These items are used as item/fluid filters, meant to be inserted into things like [Web Ports](@critterworks:web_port).

Interact with a Filterfeeder while holding any type of Sand to feed it. Each piece of Sand has a 10% chance to produce a [Pearl](@critterworks:pearl). Feeding it does not catch the Filterfeeder.

They spawn in small groups, with 20% of them being Fluid Filterfeeders.

For more information on how they can be used, see the two linked item pages.
