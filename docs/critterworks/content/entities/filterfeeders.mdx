Filterfeeders are an animal that can be found swimming along the bottom of oceans and rivers. Swimming near one will clear your vision of water fog, allowing you to see farther.

They come in two types: [Item Filterfeeders](@critterworks:item_filterfeeder) and [Fluid Filterfeeders](@critterworks:fluid_filterfeeder). These items are used as item/fluid filters, meant to be inserted into things like [Web Ports](@critterworks:web_port).

Interact with a Filterfeeder while holding any type of Sand to feed it. Each piece of Sand has a 10% chance to produce a [Pearl](@critterworks:pearl).

They spawn in small groups, with 20% of them being Fluid Filterfeeders. Use a [Critter Catcher](@critterworks:critter_catcher) on an adult Filterfeeder to catch it and convert it into the appropriate item. Drop the item into water to turn it back into a mob.

For more information on how they can be used, see the two linked item pages.
